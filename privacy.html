Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-18

The sam-gws-personal Google Cloud project authorizes a small set of personal command-line tools — an rclone Google Drive mount and the gws CLI — to access one Google account: the developer's own. There is no other user.

Data accessed: Google Drive files, Gmail messages, and Google Calendar events belonging to that single account, solely so the account owner can read, edit, and manage their own data from the command line on their own computer.

Data sharing: No data is shared with, sold to, or made accessible to any third party. Nothing is transmitted to any server other than Google's own APIs and the account owner's local machine.

Data retention: No copy of this data is retained beyond what the account owner's own local tools cache temporarily on their own device.
